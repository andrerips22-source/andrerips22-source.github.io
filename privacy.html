Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

rips-clips ("the tool") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the "Andre | Pokemon pulls" accounts, with that owner's own accounts.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the owner's channel details, video list, video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports; permission to upload videos and manage comments on the owner's channel.
  • TikTok: the owner's basic profile, profile statistics and video list; permission to send videos the owner made to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Instagram: the owner's professional account insights and media; permission to publish the owner's Reels.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform. It is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored locally and are used only to call the official APIs above.

rips-clips' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Other people

The tool may read public comments that other people leave on the owner's videos, so the owner can read and reply to them. Those comments are not used for anything else.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and in the app settings of TikTok and Instagram. To ask for data deletion, email the address below; locally stored data is deleted within 30 days.
